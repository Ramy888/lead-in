    Welcome to Lead-In

    Keep viewers watching from one show to the next

    Free streaming TV channels (FAST, like Pluto TV, Tubi or Samsung TV Plus) lose viewers whenever the next show doesn't suit the audience of the last one.

    Lead-In asks Qloo how much each show's audience likes every other show, then orders a 24-hour day so each show hands its audience to the next, and shows where it can't.

    What comes from where

    Qloo says
    How strongly fans of one show also like another (Qloo affinity, shown raw in the matrix tooltips), each show's aggregate audience skew (age, gender), and titles that share an audience with your library. These are aggregate affinities, never facts about an individual viewer.
    Our read
    The handoff score: Qloo's affinities sit in a narrow band (about 0.76 for unrelated shows to 0.97 for close neighbours), so Lead-In maps 0.86 and below to 0 and 0.975 to 1; a raw affinity under about 0.90 reads as a weak handoff. Also who typically watches in each daypart, how a show's skew fits that, the cut-off for a weak handoff, and the scheduling itself. These are Lead-In's assumptions, open to argument.
    Agent
    Claude (Anthropic) decides what to investigate and what to test, using only the tools above: Qloo lookups and what-if re-plans. It must quote only numbers the tools returned, and it files them under "Qloo says" or "Our read".
    TMDB
    Episode runtimes, which set slot lengths (runtime plus about 12% ad load, rounded up to half hours).

    How the day is built

    1. Resolve each title to a Qloo entity. Ambiguous titles ask you to choose.
    2. Get each show's audience skew and score its fit for every daypart.
    3. Build a one-way overlap matrix: for each show, ask Qloo how much its fans like every other show in the library (one call per show).
    4. Search for the 24h loop with the best total of handoffs plus daypart fit. Every show airs at least once (when the library fits), and runs of the same show are capped.
    5. Flag weak handoffs and dayparts the library serves poorly, then ask Qloo which titles would fill them.

    Ask the agent

    Claude reads your goal, asks Qloo for titles that bridge weak spots, tests what-ifs on the schedule and recommends a change, or none.
